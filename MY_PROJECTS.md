# My GenArt2025 Visualizations

Last updated: October 26, 2025

## Current Project: V3 - Ethereal Transitions + Light Trails

**What's in it:**
- 10 audio-reactive visualizations
- Tempo-based aesthetic system (country → urban transitions)
- All respond to BPM, pitch, and audio features
- Long-exposure light trail effects

**Visualizations Available:**

### Light Trails (Inspired by Long-Exposure Photography)
1. **💡 Light Highway** - Multiple colored light sources with glowing trails, like highway traffic at night
   - Bass = thick yellow/white trails (slow flowing)
   - Mid = blue/cyan trails (medium speed)
   - High = red/magenta trails (fast, erratic)
   - Very slow fade creates motion blur effect

2. **🎨 Neon Painter** - Simulates painting with light in 3D space
   - Painter "draws" smooth bezier curve strokes
   - Audio controls brush thickness, color, and movement
   - Creates abstract light sculptures
   - Current stroke glows brighter

3. **🌊 Chromatic Flow** - Flowing ribbons of color like aurora borealis
   - Smooth undulating waves across the screen
   - Multiple sine waves for organic flow
   - Bass = thick slow ribbons, Treble = thin fast ribbons
   - Heavy glow creates silk-like appearance

### Geometric Patterns
4. **🌟 Spiral Galaxy** - Multi-arm spirals growing from center, octave-based colors
5. **🌳 Fractal Tree** - Pitch-responsive branching, grows from bottom
6. **🔷 Chromatic Kaleidoscope** - Radial symmetry patterns, harmony-based complexity

### Attractors & Particles
7. **Lorenz Attractor** - 3D strange attractor with thick trails and glow
8. **Particle Field** - Motion blur trails, multi-color particles
9. **Rössler Attractor** - 3D rotating attractor, starts large
10. **Audio Waveform** - Direct audio visualization with spectrum

**How to use:**
- Run: `npm run dev`
- Open: http://localhost:5173
- Select visualization from dropdown
- Load audio file and press Play
